# Demo video script (≈4 minutes)

Recording tips: browser at 1440×900, zoom 100%, RU interface. Before recording, open the app in one tab with a **fresh account registered**, and the **demo account** in a second (private) window. Text in *italics* is what you say.

| Time | Screen | Action | Voice-over |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:30 | Landing page | Scroll slowly: hero → three modules → pricing | *Kelisim helps small importers in Kazakhstan. Aigerim buys children's clothing in Guangzhou. A lawyer costs up to 150 thousand tenge per contract, templates from chats ignore currency control, and the real losses come after signing: a missing UCN, a late repatriation, or a sanctioned supplier. Kelisim solves this with three modules: documents, risk check and deadline tracking.* |
| 0:30–1:00 | Register → Onboarding | Register with a phone; enter BIN `950312400129` → "We found your company" → bank details → skip Telegram → empty dashboard | *Sign-up takes a phone and a password. The BIN is validated and the company is filled in automatically; bank details go into every document. A new user sees an empty dashboard with one clear action.* |
| 1:00–1:50 | Wizard | "Create first deal" → step 1 (supply, RU+CN) → step 2: click "Next" empty to show the **validation error**, then "Fill with example" → step 3 goods and total 18,400 USD → step 4 FCA Guangzhou, 100% prepayment, dates; point at "Draft saved" and the "?" hints | *The deal form has four short steps. Errors are shown next to the field; the draft is saved automatically and every legal term has a plain-language hint.* |
| 1:50–2:20 | Check → Result | Press "Check": three checks run one after another → **green** result | *The compliance engine screens the supplier against the real OFAC, EU and UK sanctions lists, updated every day, checks whether a UCN is needed — 50,000 USD at the official National Bank rate on the signing date — and calculates the repatriation term. Here everything is green; the footer shows which list version and rate were used.* |
| 2:20–3:00 | Package preview | Switch RU / CN / bilingual; open the invoice and packing list; change payment to 30% in the clause panel → "Apply"; Download → "Whole package · DOCX" → success modal "Deadlines added" → "Later" | *Four documents from one form, Russian and Chinese side by side. Changing a clause rebuilds the whole package. Downloading saves the deal and puts its deadlines into the calendar.* |
| 3:00–3:30 | Deal page, Counterparties, red case | Show tabs of the deal; in Counterparties add `Shenzhen Redline Electronics Co., Ltd.` → **red**, "found on the EU list"; mention yellow = UCN (show "Нужен УНК" badge in the demo account next) | *If the supplier is on a sanctions list, the result is red with the source list, and documents are blocked until the data is changed.* |
| 3:30–4:10 | Demo account | Dashboard with overdue banner and "UCN needed"; open Shenzhen deal → enter a UCN; Calendar → overdue red card → "Mark as done" with a proof note and attach a PDF (bank statement); Settings → Telegram and email reminders; switch dark theme and EN; on a phone: install the app to the home screen and open the three-dot menu | *Yerzhan's view: the dashboard shows what needs attention. He enters the UCN from the bank, marks the overdue delivery as done and attaches the bank statement, and the Telegram bot will remind him 7, 3 and 1 day before each deadline. The interface works in Russian and English and on mobile.* |
| 4:10–4:30 | README / code | Show project structure, `npm test` passing, Cloudflare deploy | *The MVP runs on Cloudflare Workers with a D1 database, deploys automatically from GitHub, imports sanctions lists daily, and has 47 unit tests plus an end-to-end test of the whole journey. Next steps: legal review of templates, payments and a B2B workspace.* |
