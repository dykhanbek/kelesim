# Demo video script (≈4.5 minutes)

Recording tips: browser at 1440×900, zoom 100%, RU interface. Before recording, open the app in one tab with a **fresh account registered**, and the **demo account** in a second (private) window. Text in *italics* is what you say.

| Time | Screen | Action | Voice-over |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:30 | Landing page | Scroll slowly: hero «ВЭД без юридических сюрпризов» → four problems → four modules | *Kelisim is a risk-management platform for international deals of small and medium businesses in Kazakhstan. Aigerim imports children's clothing from Guangzhou. Her real risk is not the paperwork — it's a lost prepayment, a payment blocked because of sanctions, a currency-control fine or goods stuck at customs. Kelisim checks all of that before she sends the money.* |
| 0:30–0:55 | Register → Onboarding | Register with a phone (three unticked consent boxes); enter BIN `950312400129` → "We found your company" → bank details → skip Telegram | *Sign-up asks for separate consents as Kazakh law requires. The company is found by BIN and its details go into every document.* |
| 0:55–1:45 | Wizard | Step 1 → step 2: click "Next" empty to show the **validation error**, then "Fill with example" — point at **Owners and director** (beneficiary + director) → step 3: goods with HS codes, «for children» ticked → step 4: payment, **advance guarantee**, **route and end user** | *Four short steps. Besides the supplier we record who owns it — from the registry extract — and where the goods go. That is what banks ask about today.* |
| 1:45–2:15 | Check → Result + readiness card | Press "Check": checks run → **green** result; under it the **Deal readiness** card | *The supplier and each owner are screened against OFAC, EU, UK and UN lists and politically exposed persons, updated daily. The goods are checked against the CHPL export-control list and the route. Plus UCN and repatriation at the official National Bank rate.* |
| 2:15–2:45 | Package preview | Switch RU / CN / bilingual; Download → "Whole package · DOCX" → "Deadlines added" | *Four bilingual documents from one form; downloading puts the deadlines into the calendar.* |
| 2:45–3:40 | Demo account → deal KLS-003 → **Readiness** tab | Show the score (≈59/100), five areas, "What lowers the score" (−10 prepayment without guarantee, −10 UCN…); scroll to the **import checklist**: ТР ТС 007/2011, footwear marking since 2021 → click "Done" on the certificate → score goes up. Open **Check** tab → yellow "politically exposed person in the structure". Back to Readiness → Owners → add parent `Shenzhen Redline Electronics Co., Ltd.` → Save → **"Blocking risk"** | *Every deal gets a readiness score from 0 to 100 across five areas, and every lost point has a reason and a next step. The checklist tells what customs will need for these exact HS codes — certificates under EAEU regulations and Kazakhstan's mandatory marking. If the supplier's parent company is sanctioned, the deal turns red even though the supplier itself is clean — the 50% ownership rule.* |
| 3:40–4:10 | Calendar, Settings, phone | Overdue card → "Mark as done" + PDF; Settings → Telegram bot, Data & privacy; switch to Қазақша and dark theme | *Deadlines with Telegram reminders, personal-data controls, and the interface in Russian, Kazakh and English.* |
| 4:10–4:30 | README / code | Project structure, `npm test` passing, GitHub Actions deploy | *The MVP runs on Cloudflare Workers with D1, deploys from GitHub, imports sanctions and PEP data daily, and has 75 unit tests plus an end-to-end test of the whole journey. Next: paid data providers for beneficial ownership and court records, AI contract review and payments.* |
